a) Agile Development
  Pros: Fast pasted, allowed greater collaboration because everything is broken into smaller groups
  Cons: Harder to work in large groups because of the needed coordination and quick turn arounds, the structure allows for the ability to go back and fix things each time to make them better but then you don't really have an end point
b) Scrum Master vs. Project Owner
  Scrum Master: Is not the project owner but they often work together. The scrum master is someone who's focus is on the scrum and making sure the rest of the team also follows. Help people manage themself and get this things done without them needed to be perfect.
  Project Owner: The project owner is incharge of the entire project and its end goal. They need to make sure everything stays on track over all.
c) Types of Sprint Meetings:
    Sprint planning: Make sure everyone knows whats going on, everyone decides what they want to do
    Daily Scrum: Identify issues, ask for help when needed
    Sprint Review: Present completed work and ask for feedback, discuss issues going into next week
d) Issue types:
    Epics: A larger feature containing multiple parts. Needs many people and stories to complete it.
    Stories: A singular larger task one person can take on over the week. Instead of multiple people with a large amount of parts.
    Bug: A tiny issue that needs to be fixed as soon as possible before more progress can be made
    Task: One singular quick task. Less than a story but larger and not as glaring as a bug.
